      Summary

      States are inferred from motion and breathing by fixed rules. They are probabilities about moments, not diagnoses, and the hiring judgement stays with you.

      Nothing is recorded until the candidate agrees in the headset. Raw audio never leaves the headset; raw motion is reduced to per-second measurements and discarded. Candidates appear under pseudonyms and can ask for their data to be deleted.
